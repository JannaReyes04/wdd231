NP Membership

Designed for nonprofit organizations serving Meycauayan City.

  • No membership fee
  • Chamber directory listing
  • Community networking opportunities
  • Access to selected chamber events

Bronze Membership

Designed for businesses beginning their involvement with the chamber.

  • Business directory listing
  • Networking events
  • Business training opportunities
  • Member event discounts

Silver Membership

Provides additional promotional opportunities for growing businesses.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Enhanced directory listing
  • Additional event discounts
  • Business spotlight opportunities
  • Training and workshops

Gold Membership

Provides our highest level of visibility and chamber benefits.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Premium directory listing
  • Priority event discounts
  • Homepage spotlight opportunities
  • Advertising opportunities
  • Priority chamber event participation
